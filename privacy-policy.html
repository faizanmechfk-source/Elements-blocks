Official Privacy Policy

ELEMENT BLOCKS

Developer: YSF Tech  |  Package Name: com.ysftech.elementblocks  |  Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Overview & 100% Offline Operation

Element Blocks ("the Game"), developed by YSF Tech, is a completely offline puzzle game designed for Android devices. We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency.

We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data or user information to any external servers or third parties. The Game does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Because Element Blocks operates entirely offline on your device, we never collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials
  • Precise or approximate geographic location data
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, Advertising ID, Android ID, MAC address)
  • Contacts, photos, media files, microphone audio, or camera inputs
  • Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioral tracking data

3. Local Device Storage

To provide seamless gameplay, Element Blocks stores your game progress strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and local WebView storage (localStorage):

  • High scores, current board state, and gameplay statistics
  • Collected Stars, Power-Up inventory, and unlocked Achievements
  • Daily Challenge progress and Consecutive Days Played streak counters
  • Audio, Music, Vibration, and Haptic feedback preferences

Full User Control: This data never leaves your device. You can erase all saved game data and statistics at any time inside the Game via Settings → Reset All Statistics & Progress, or by clearing the app's storage in your Android system settings.

4. Device Permissions

Element Blocks adheres to the principle of least privilege and requests only one install-time permission:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide optional tactile vibration and haptic feedback during block placement, line clears, and power-up effects. You can disable Vibration and Haptic Feedback at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Advertising

Element Blocks contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no real-money in-app billing. All in-game power-ups are unlocked using Stars earned purely through gameplay, achievements, and daily challenges.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Element Blocks is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Because zero personal data is collected or transmitted, the Game fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR provisions for minors.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of Element Blocks, we will revise the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and include the updated notice within the application.
