    How to Play

    1. Place Elemental Blocks

    Touch, hold, and drag any of the 3 pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 grid. Blocks stay exactly where placed—there is no gravity.

    2. Clear Full Rows & Columns

    Fill any complete horizontal row or vertical column of 8 cells to clear it! Clear multiple lines in one move for massive combo bonuses.

    3. Build Your Multiplier

    Consecutive turns with line clears boost your score multiplier up to 10x+. Moves without a clear gradually step down your streak.

    4. Collect Stars & Use Power-Ups

    Clear lines passing through ★ cells to earn Stars. Spend Stars in the Shop or during gameplay for Dynamite (3×3 blast), Lightning (element wipe), Hammer (single cell), or Shuffle (new tray).

    5. Four Elemental Themes

    🔥 Fire 💧 Water 🌿 Earth 💨 Air

    All elements share identical placement rules—master their board synergy!
