# Privacy Policy for Element Blocks

- **Application Name:** Element Blocks
- **Package Name:** `com.ysftech.elementblocks`
- **Developer:** YSF Tech
- **Effective Date:** November 17, 2026

---

## 1. Overview & 100% Offline Operation
**Element Blocks** ("the Game"), developed by **YSF Tech**, is a standalone, offline puzzle game for Android. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

**We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data or user information to any external servers or third parties.** The Game does not request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission and performs zero network requests.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect
Because Element Blocks operates 100% offline on your device, we never collect:
- Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, or account login)
- Precise or approximate location data
- Device identifiers (Advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI, IP address, or MAC address)
- Photos, media, files, contacts, microphone audio, or camera data
- Usage telemetry, analytics, or crash reports

## 3. Local Device Storage
To save your progress between sessions, Element Blocks stores game data strictly locally on your device using Android `SharedPreferences` and WebView `localStorage`:
- High scores, current board state, and gameplay statistics
- Star balance, Power-Up inventory, and unlocked Achievements
- Offline Daily Challenge progress and Consecutive Days Played streak counters
- Sound, Music, Vibration, and Haptic feedback settings

**User Control & Data Deletion:** Your progress data never leaves your device. You can permanently delete and reset all saved game data at any time from within the app by navigating to **Settings → Reset All Statistics & Progress**, or by clearing the application data in your Android device settings.

## 4. Permissions Used
Element Blocks requests only a single non-sensitive, install-time Android permission:
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Used solely to provide optional vibration and haptic feedback during gameplay (such as block placement, line clears, and power-up effects). You can turn off Vibration and Haptic Feedback at any time in the in-game **Settings** screen.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising
Element Blocks does **not** integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics services, or real-money billing systems. All power-ups and rewards are earned through in-game Stars.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)
Element Blocks is safe for players of all ages. We do not knowingly or unknowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region).

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the Game. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
